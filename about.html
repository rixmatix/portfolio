About

I build the systems behind the screens.

I'm Ricky — a design systems leader in Chicago with 15+ years designing complex, data-heavy products across fintech, analytics, and healthcare education.

I've built systems from zero — Liquid at M1 Finance, BMW's first internal pattern library — governed them at scale, and connected design to engineering through tokens, Storybook, and Figma-to-code workflows.

Lately I've been integrating AI into design operations: using Claude and Figma's MCP to accelerate documentation, audit token architecture, and make design systems machine-readable as well as human-usable.

I still believe what got me into this: design changes outcomes — for the business, and for the person on the other side of the screen. Systems are just how you make that change stick.

How I work

Four steps, on repeat

  • step/01

    Define

    Understand why it matters before deciding what to build. What problem are we solving, and what does it unlock for the business?

  • step/02

    Understand

    The people using it — and the teams maintaining it. Both audiences shape what "good" means.

  • step/03

    Design

    Tangible solutions, shared early, refined in the open. The fastest way to improve design is to show it to others.

  • step/04

    Validate

    Test, measure adoption, evolve. A system is never done — it's living infrastructure.
